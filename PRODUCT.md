# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. The site must open directly from `index.html` without a build step.

## Users

Women in the user's office are the recipients; office colleagues open the page as a shared 20/10 greeting.

## Product Purpose

A one-page digital gift for Vietnamese Women's Day (20 October), celebrating the women in the office. Success means the recipients feel appreciated, enjoy the surprises, and want to share or capture the experience.

## Positioning

A personal office greeting built around the real portraits supplied with this project, rather than a generic celebration template.

## Operating Context

Recipients view the page on desktop, tablet, or mobile, potentially by opening a shared link. The page should be usable directly from its local `index.html`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive single-page experience with interactive wishes, flower surprise, photo gallery and lightbox, and celebration effects.
- Use the supplied portraits in personalized sections; retain the corresponding names from the filenames.
- Do not require a framework, build step, or unsupplied audio file. Audio is optional and must be user-controlled.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep effects performant on mobile.
- Deployment destination and office/team name are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

For this occasion, use the user's requested Elegant Feminine, Romantic, Modern Office Celebration tone: warm, joyful, affectionate, polished, and festive without visual clutter. Pink/rose, champagne gold, cream, and white are the specified color family.

No music file, logo, office name, or event photos are supplied; do not imply otherwise.

## Product Principles

- Make the greeting feel made for this office.
- Celebrate recipients warmly and inclusively.
- Keep interactions clear, delightful, and easy to use.
- Preserve responsive, accessible, and performant behavior.
